Privacy Policy
RDS Companion
Last updated: March 25, 2026

RDS Companion does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no network communication of any kind.

Data Storage

Any data generated or saved by RDS Companion — such as user preferences or app state — is stored exclusively on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Third-Party SDKs

RDS Companion uses only Apple's standard iOS frameworks. No third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising libraries are included.

Children's Privacy

RDS Companion does not collect any data from any users, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to rds.companion@gmail.com.
